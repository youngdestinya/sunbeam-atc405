ADR-01 · Managed PaaS for readings

Status: accepted proposal; not provisioned. Context: 19.2 million readings/day at the assigned production tier, bursty mobile delivery, nightly reconciliation and a three-engineer team.

Decision: managed ingestion/queueing, transformations and query services. Preserve the pseudonymized South African lake, subject to documented transfer safeguards. Put event-time outage alerting on an independent Nigerian path, subject to verified service availability.

Alternatives: IaaS shifts patching and platform operation to Sunbeam; self-managed Kubernetes/Kafka are not justified. FaaS alone does not resolve durable state and replay.

Consequences: reduce operating toil; retain provider dependence and foreign-exchange exposure. Sunbeam owns access, schema, quality, lawful processing, recovery tests and alert evidence. Use event-time windows, deduplication and dead-letter handling. Monitor end-to-end delay.

ADR-02 · Keep the identity key in Abeokuta

Status: accepted architectural refusal; vault is outside this demo. Context: re-identification enables household surveillance; pseudonymized consumption remains personal data.

Decision: retain the encrypted identity mapping and keys on Sunbeam-controlled infrastructure in Abeokuta, with restricted network access, separation of duties, approved case-based retrieval and tested encrypted backups. Do not move the mapping into the foreign lake.

Tradeoff: Sunbeam retains local host, patching, physical security, backup and recovery work. Assign ownership among existing engineers; location by itself does not prove compliance.
